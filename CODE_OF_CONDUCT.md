# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project discussions, issues, reviews, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions and restrict participation fairly and proportionately. No contributor is owed adoption of a change or unlimited maintainer time. If misconduct includes private information, do not repeat it publicly. A private reporting contact has not been configured; the maintainer must select one before inviting private reports.

This short project-specific policy is original text, not a claim to adopt a third-party code of conduct or its enforcement infrastructure.
